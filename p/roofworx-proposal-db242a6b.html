The problem

Roofworx does $263k/month in revenue with 5 reps closing 24 to 25 jobs. The operation works. What does not work is knowing where the profit actually comes from

$23,000 leaves the account every month across six marketing channels. There is no single view that shows which channels produce closed jobs and which ones burn cash. Decisions about where to spend more or less are based on gut feel, not data. That lack of transparency is costing tens of thousands per year in misallocated spend

    Job Nimbus sync

    How data moves between Job Nimbus and the dashboard

    Tier 1: One-way. Dashboard reads JN data every 15 minutes via polling. You can view JN data in the dashboard but cannot push changes back to JN from it

    Tier 2: One-way with webhook and polling fallback. Dashboard updates within seconds when JN changes. Cannot push changes back to JN

    Tier 3: Two-way, real time. Webhook-based so when something changes in JN the dashboard updates within seconds. Dashboard can also trigger status updates in JN (automated stage transitions, tagging). No manual refresh needed

    Tier 4: Same as Tier 3

    Pipeline automation

    Automated stage transitions, alerts, and routing that run behind the scenes

    Tier 1: Sync only. JN status changes show on the dashboard. No automated transitions, alerts, or routing

    Tier 2: System triggered, confirmed by team. Stale deal alerts, new lead auto-sync, email alerts to Daria for flagged items. The system prepares and flags, your team confirms. Runs on Make.com at approximately $10 per month

    Tier 3: Full automation. 9 automation scenarios. New lead auto-sync from marketing channels, stale deal alerts (jobs not updated in 7+ days), system-triggered stage transitions (e.g., estimate created triggers status change, confirmed by team), email alerts to Daria for flagged items, payment detection, contract detection. Includes AI-powered email classification: when a customer replies to an estimate, the system detects their intent and suggests the correct next pipeline stage, with human confirmation before anything moves. Runs on Make.com at approximately $10 per month

    Tier 4: Same as Tier 3 (full automation with AI-powered email classification). Nothing changes in the pipeline without a human in the loop
